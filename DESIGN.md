## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, boutique medical-spa aesthetic tailored for modern aesthetic medicine and holistic wellness in Tierra del Fuego. The experience deliberately rejects cold, clinical sterility in favor of serene luxury, organic balance, and calming sanctuary.

Key emotional attributes:
- **Serenity & Warmth:** Soft organic tints, warm off-white foundations, and open space that evoke breathing room and mindful relaxation.
- **Understated Elegance:** Precision typography paired with generous negative space and delicate accents rather than heavy decorative elements.
- **Reassurance & Clinical Trust:** High visual polish, effortless readability, and refined structure that communicate medical-grade expertise without clinical anxiety.

The aesthetic philosophy draws from modern minimalism and tactile glassmorphic accents, utilizing subtle atmospheric backdrops, gentle pill-shaped containers, and ultra-soft tonal shadows.

## Colors

The palette is rooted directly in the brand identity, balancing calming mineral tones with comforting organic hues:

- **Primary (`#4BAE9C` / `#BDE8DE` mint tints):** Represents clinical freshness, rejuvenation, and cellular balance. Used for primary CTAs, active states, key highlight pills, and logo-aligned accents.
- **Secondary (`#DFC2B8` / `#E8C5B8` warm nude rose):** Adds human tenderness, skincare tactility, and boutique warmth. Used for subtle background banners, secondary buttons, tags, and accent borders.
- **Tertiary (`#503D6B` deep plum/violet):** Drawn from the mark's distinct accent dot, providing high-prestige grounding for selective micro-badges, luxury markers, and deep emphasis.
- **Neutral (`#F9F5F1` warm beige canvas & `#FFFFFF` pure surface):** Forms the gentle, cocooning background canvas. Replaces aggressive pure-white page backgrounds with a soothing sand-linen tone, using pure white (`#FFFFFF`) strictly for elevated cards.
- **Text & Contrast (`#1A1A1A` soft black & `#5E5752` warm slate):** Ensures WCAG AAA reading contrast without the harshness of pure ink black.

## Typography

The type system blends the refined geometric elegance of **Montserrat** for display and section titles with the warm, open humanist legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body copy and technical treatment details.

- **Headlines & Subtitles:** Set in Montserrat with optical kerning and measured tracking. Subtitles and section overlines utilize uppercase tracking (`+0.08em` to `+0.12em`) to introduce a light editorial rhythm.
- **Body & Captions:** Handled by Plus Jakarta Sans to maximize effortless reading of treatment descriptions, procedural care guidelines, and testimonials across screen sizes.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Never use weights heavier than 600 in display sizes; sophistication is preserved through light and regular cuts framed with generous spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces expansive white space, emulating the open, tranquil environment of a premium spa clinic:

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views operate on a 12-column fluid grid capped at a maximum container width of `1280px`. Tablet transitions to an 8-column format, while mobile uses a 4-column layout with safe 20px padding.
- **Section Pacing:** Vertical pacing between thematic sections (e.g., Treatments, Philosophy, Specialist Team, Booking) uses generous pauses (`space-xl` scaled up to `6rem` to `8rem` on desktop), establishing an unhurried, calm tempo.
- **Breathing Envelopes:** Treatment and benefit cards maintain internal padding of at least `2rem` (`space-lg`), preventing clinical clutter and maintaining visual quietness.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids abrupt or industrial drop-shadows. Depth is achieved via diffuse, ambient atmospheric layering:

- **Canvas & Surface Tiers:** The base level is warm sand (`#F9F5F1`). Treatment cards, consultation panels, and interactive elements rest on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted mint-frost cards (`#FFFFFF` with 80% opacity and 12px backdrop blur).
- **Soft Ambient Shading:** Shadows are ultra-diffused, tinted with warm neutral pigment rather than dead grey:
  - *Resting card:* `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(80, 61, 107, 0.04), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.02)`
  - *Floating / Hover:* `0 20px 40px -12px rgba(75, 174, 156, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.03)`
- **Hairline Tactile Borders:** Cards and inputs carry a translucent hairline border (`1px solid rgba(223, 194, 184, 0.45)`) that defines silhouettes gently without visual noise.

## Shapes

The form language is organic and soft, reflecting natural contours, face shapes, and calm flowing textures:

- **Card Silhouettes:** Generously rounded corners (`rounded-2xl` / `rounded-3xl` corresponding to 24px - 32px radii) soften structural borders.
- **Interactive Triggers:** Primary buttons, category tags, and status pills adopt full pill morphology (`rounded-full` / 9999px), providing an inviting, tactile touch-target.
- **Image Treatment:** Photography of clinical procedures, botanicals, and interiors is framed with pill-shaped masks or organic asymmetric pebble radii to soften the medical focus.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Action (Agendar Turno / Consultar):** Full pill shape, filled with `#4BAE9C`, text in pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with medium weight. Hover shifts into an organic lift with a delicate mint glow (`box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -6px rgba(75, 174, 156, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Action (Conocer Tratamientos):** Translucent nude-rose outline (`#DFC2B8`) or solid soft tint (`rgba(223, 194, 184, 0.25)`) with soft black typography.
- **Text Link:** Understated with an animated expanding underline in primary mint.

### Treatment & Service Cards
- White background (`#FFFFFF`) encapsulated in 24px corner radius.
- Subtle inner padding of 32px.
- Category tag styled as an ultra-subtle pill at the top left using the nude or mint tint.
- Gentle micro-interaction on hover: smooth translateY(-4px) transition over 300ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1).

### Consultation Form Inputs
- Rounded pill or soft capsule (`rounded-full` or `rounded-xl`).
- Background: `#FFFFFF` with hairline nude border (`#DFC2B8`).
- Focus state: Ring glow in soft mint (`rgba(75, 174, 156, 0.25)`) with no sharp default outline.
- Floating micro-labels in uppercase letter-spaced typography.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Used for filtering clinical categories (Facial, Corporal, Medicina Estética, Relax).
- Inactive: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `rgba(223, 194, 184, 0.6)`, text `#5E5752`.
- Active: Background `#4BAE9C`, border `#4BAE9C`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Testimonial & Medical Assurance Badges
- Floating glassmorphic widgets featuring the deep plum accent dot (`#503D6B`) as a mark of certified medical quality and patient verification.
- Backdrop blur of `16px` over lifestyle photography for subtle contrast.
